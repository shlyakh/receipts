# Primary comparison (scored set)

**JC: inconclusive.** On JC, a week ahead with archived forecast weather, Chronos-2's error was 0.817 of the temperature-only lag regression's (90% interval 0.686 to 0.960). The interval crosses the 10% bar, so 48 weeks cannot separate a tie from a real difference. 48 of 48 scored weeks start after the date of the pinned weights. Earlier years of the same public series may be in the model's training data.

**DOM: worth_switching.** On DOM, a week ahead with archived forecast weather, Chronos-2's error was 0.730 of the temperature-only lag regression's (90% interval 0.665 to 0.805). The whole interval is below 0.90, so the model clears the 10% bar. 48 of 48 scored weeks start after the date of the pinned weights. Earlier years of the same public series may be in the model's training data.
